Perforate privacy

Effective August 12, 2026.

Perforate is a visual sticker editor that runs inside ChatGPT. It does not ask for or store your OpenAI password, API key, payment information, or ChatGPT account credentials.

Data handled

Your sticker recipe, canvas positions, and imported image references are used only to provide the editor. Canvas state is saved in your browser or ChatGPT widget state. Perforate has no separate user database.

ChatGPT generation

When you choose Generate, your assembled art brief is posted into the current ChatGPT conversation. Image generation and related data handling are provided by ChatGPT under your OpenAI account and settings.

Retention and control

You can remove stickers in the editor, clear site data in your browser, or delete the associated ChatGPT conversation and files using ChatGPT controls.

Contact

Questions can be filed in the project’s GitHub repository.
